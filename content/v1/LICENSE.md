These files are generated by `scripts/content/build.py` in
https://github.com/BARDAMRI/LinguaAdapt and may be reused under the licenses below.

## dictionary.json, words-en-he.json, words-he-en.json

Adapted from the English Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org), by Wiktionary
contributors, via wiktextract / kaikki.org (https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Hebrew/).
Changes: entries were filtered, glosses shortened, frequency ranks and course
order added, and missing vowel signs added automatically.
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). These adapted files are
released under the same license.

## sentences.json

Sentences and translations from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), by Tatoeba
contributors. Each sentence keeps its Tatoeba ids in `src` ([Hebrew, English]);
the author of a sentence is listed on https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/<id>.
Changes: sentences were selected and vowel signs (niqqud) were added
automatically with DictaBERT-large-char-menaked by Dicta (CC BY 4.0,
https://huggingface.co/dicta-il/dictabert-large-char-menaked).
License: Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/).
Sentences marked `"own": true` were written for LinguaAdapt.
