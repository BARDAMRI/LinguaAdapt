2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
LinguaAdapt is a free course for complete beginners. English speakers learn to read and understand Hebrew from zero: the alphabet, the vowel marks (niqqud), common words and then real sentences. Hebrew speakers learn English the same way, from the 26 letters and phonics to words and sentences. It solves the first hurdle of a new language, a new script: letters come in small steps, every word is read aloud by natural recorded voices, and spaced repetition brings words back before they are forgotten. No ads, no subscription, no account. Audience: adults and teens starting Hebrew or English.

3. HOW TO USE IT
No sign-in, login credentials or sample files are needed. On first launch:
- Choose a course: "I speak English / Learn Hebrew" or "I speak Hebrew / Learn English".
- Choose "From the very beginning" (or "I know some already" to take a placement checkpoint).
- On the home screen, tap the current lesson. Lessons mix listening, matching, spelling with letter tiles, building sentences and typing.
- From the home screen: Dictionary (search in Hebrew or English), Phrasebook (169 phrases in 17 situations), Letter chart, Profile (XP, streaks, lessons done) and Settings (course, daily goal, slow speech, sound, niqqud, transliteration, app language, dark mode, Credits & licenses, Privacy). Words saved from the dictionary can be practiced under "My words".
The first launch needs an internet connection to download the word lists and recordings. Downloaded lessons keep working offline.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- GitHub Pages (https://bardamri.github.io/LinguaAdapt): static hosting for the word lists, sentences and pre-recorded audio that the app downloads over HTTPS. Nothing about the user is sent.
- Apple's on-device text-to-speech (AVSpeechSynthesizer), used only where a recording is missing.
The app uses no login, analytics, advertising, payment or AI services. Progress is stored only on the device.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app and its content are the same in every region. The interface is in English or Hebrew: it follows the device language and can be changed in Settings.
